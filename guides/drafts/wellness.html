2. Protect your sleep

The CDC recommends at least 7 hours a night for adults 18 to 60.23 With little ones that may be out of reach some seasons, so protect what you can: split nights, trade one “off” night a week, go to bed with the kids sometimes.

Minimum: one early night this week.

3. Easy fuel

Pick a no-decision breakfast and lunch you can repeat. Keep snacks where you actually are: the car, the diaper bag, your desk.

Minimum: eat something real before noon.

4. Movement that fits

A stroller walk, a few stretches during bath time, a dance party in the kitchen. Movement you will do beats a plan you won’t.

Minimum: five minutes outside.

5. Your own appointments

Your physical, dentist, eyes, women’s health and mental health check-ins. Add them to the yearly family meeting so they get booked, and set prescription refill reminders a week early.

Minimum: book the one you’ve been putting off.

If ADHD is in your house

Tie each routine to something you already do every day: water next to the coffee maker, a stretch while the shower warms up. Put refills and appointments on automatic reminders. Expect to restart often, and build the restart in.
