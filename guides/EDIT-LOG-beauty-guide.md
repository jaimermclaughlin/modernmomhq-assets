Foundation can be mixed with other foundations

Foundation colors can be blended to create your own perfect color match

Don't ever use your fingers or hands to apply foundation

"Primer" is not always needed! ( I actually do not prefer primer at all!)
Your foundation will only take you so far - if your skin is in bad shape, no foundation

BETWEEN BRONZER + CONTOUR

There is a lot of confusion with the difference between bronzer and contour. But rest assured,
they are really similar and do a similar job. A contour is typically a matte powder or cream
that is cut into the check, down the sides of the nose (more advanced application) and on

sort of shimmer in them to create a glow but there are matte finish bronzers available as
well.

bronzer to simply give myself a glow, but most days I like full coverage and heavier look
(that will last me all day long through work and kids), so in that case, I use a contour and a
bronzer layered.

WHAT'S THE DIFFERENCE?
This is a common question among mama’s who are a little overwhelmed in the amount of
options there are. Both Bronzer and Blush are used to add color and definition to the face.
