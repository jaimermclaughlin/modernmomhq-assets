- **Same style as the other two books:** the same fonts (including your Awesome Ways script instead of Mrs Saint Delafield), chapter openers, footers, a contents page with real page numbers, numbered sources, and the same boxes and cards. Your 34 pages are now 8 chapters: Before You Start, The Face, The Eyes, The Brows, The Lips, The Tools, Your Routine and Hair.
- **All photos kept:** your cover photo, the 9 how-to and brow photos, the makeup flat lay, and all 19 product photos (on 20 product cards, since one photo is used for two cards). The other product cards had no photo on the canvas either. Your face-map drawing is kept too.
- **New sections, from your Healthy Happy Mom course (HHM):** Why getting ready matters (Chapter 1, short, pointing to The Woman Beyond Mom for the full version), Where to spend, where to save (HHM pp. 91 to 92), The everyday face (HHM pp. 95 to 97) and a whole Hair chapter (HHM pp. 88 to 94).
- **Copyedit of the whole guide:** grammar and flow, in your voice, with contractions where you would say them (don't, it's, you're). Every changed sentence is listed below.
- **Page numbers:** the canvas pointed to pages ("see page 16"). Those now point to chapters, since page numbers change as the guide grows.
- **Product claims:** the "per the page" product details are exactly as checked on retailer pages on October 19, 2026. I couldn't reach those sites from here to check them again.

PROBLEM: SUPER OILY HAIR:
To combat this problem, you need to shampoo your hair twice and condition every
single time you wash your hair. Do not ever skip a step (some people will skip
conditioner for this reason - don't do that). Try a clarifying shampoo to help clarify
your roots and make sure you are moisturizing the mid-shaft and ends of your hair.
Typically, overactive sebaceous glands (which creates the excess oil) believe that
your hair needs more moisture, so it creates more. By training your hair to produce
less because your hair is already moisturized should help the problem.

PROBLEM: UNCONTROLLABLE/UNMANAGEABLE HAIR:
To combat this, you need to use a smoothing shampoo and conditioner and another
smoothing element for your blowouts such as a blow dry lotion or a smoothing oil.
This will help combat the frizz.

PROBLEM: SUPER THICK HAIR THAT TAKES TOO LONG:
Get regular haircuts and use a smoothing blow dry serum PLUS a blow-dry
accelerator

PROBLEM: SUPER THIN HAIR WITH NO VOLUME
Use a shampoo and conditioner that will add volume, use a root booster, and use a
round brush when blow drying. Once the hair is done, there are backcombing sprays
that will add SO much volume to your hair and help it stay volumized rather than
